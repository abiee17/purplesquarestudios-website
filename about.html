OUR STORY

Small Studio,
Big Heart

We're a tiny team who quit our day jobs to make games that feel like a weekend well spent. No investors, no crunch, no compromises — just pure indie spirit.

WHO WE ARE

We Make Games
We'd Want to Play

Purple Square Studios was born in 2025 out of a simple frustration: why were so many games stressful? We wanted games that felt like a warm hug — approachable, joyful, and impossible to put down.

We're bringing the vibrant colors, sounds, and stories of India to players around the world. Every puzzle we design is a doorway into a new culture — where gameplay meets narrative, and fun meets meaning.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Our Values

💜

Made with Love

Every game we ship is something we genuinely want to play. If we wouldn't spend it, Crafting with it, we don't release it.

✨

Joy First

No grinding, no dark patterns, no pay-to-win — the kind that makes you smile at your screen.

🎮

Players First

FOUR AND A HALF HUMANS

Meet the Team

A

Abhilasha Singh

Founder & Game Designer

The one who started it all. Armed with a Masters in Digital Game Design from NID and an unshakeable belief that games can change how people see the world. Designs every puzzle, writes every story beat, and loses sleep over whether that shade of purple is juicy enough. Fuelled by chai, gardening breaks, and long walks with two very opinionated dogs.

🎮 Learning through play, always
S

Saurabh

Tech Consultant

The person you call when the code catches fire at 2 AM. Saurabh keeps the technical engine running — from architecture decisions to performance optimizations. Has a sixth sense for bugs and an encyclopedic knowledge of Unity's darkest corners. Calm under pressure, deadly with a debugger.

🛠️ Fixes things before they break
P

Priya

Design Consultant

The eye behind the vibe. Priya makes sure every screen, every button, and every pixel feels intentional and delightful. From color palettes to typography choices, she turns good ideas into beautiful experiences. If something on screen makes you go "ooh" — that was probably Priya.

🎨 Makes pixels feel like poetry

Abhishek

Part strategist, part cheerleader, fully invested.
